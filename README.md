# Sam McNeill

Biological data scientist working at the intersection of plant breeding, statistical genetics, and applied statistics.

I have a background in plant biotechnology, genetics, and breeding, and an M.S. in Applied Statistics and Data Science from Colorado State University. My work has focused on genomic and phenotypic data, multi-environment field trials, experimental design, power simulation, and reproducible workflows for crop breeding and pathology research.

## Research interests

- Statistical genetics and quantitative genetics
- Genotype-by-environment interaction
- Experimental design and power analysis
- Genomic and phenotypic data integration
- High-throughput phenotyping
- Climate-resilient crop breeding
- Reproducible biological data science

## Selected projects

- [Genomic Variant Explorer](https://github.com/Sam-McNe/genomic-variant-explorer-USDA) — R Shiny application for exploring genomic variation in wild sugar beet accessions
- [XP-GWAS Simulation](https://github.com/Sam-McNe/XP-GWAS-Simulation-USDA) — Simulation framework for evaluating XP-GWAS study-design considerations
- [USDA Field-Trial Assessment](https://github.com/Sam-McNe/2024_RRCR_Assessment_USDA) — Reproducible analysis of multi-environment prebreeding and disease-assessment data
